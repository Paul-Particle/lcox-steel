Each route sizes its own electricity system, so LCOE is a per-route number. The two taxonomies differ only in that the alternative separates renewables capital from its fixed O&M — shown side by side anyway, so the pair can be read the same way throughout.

Every part the model actually has · € / t liquid steel

The same LCOS as above, cut as finely as the model allows — each plant's annualised capital apart from its fixed O&M, the gas bill apart from any carbon price, the electrolyser's water apart from its capital, renewables by technology, the battery's power apart from its energy, the grid connection apart from the energy it carries. Hover a segment for the quotes and quantities that produce it, its share of the group it belongs to, and its share of LCOS. Parts too thin to put a pointer on are folded into the largest band of their own group, so nothing here is unreachable.
