Reading this. LCOS is the full levelised cost per tonne of liquid steel — annualised capex plus operating cost of the whole system (renewables, storage, grid, process plant, ore and consumables) divided by annual output. Every panel compares Scenario A against Scenario B. B starts equal to A; each override you set in its strip makes B deviate on one axis, everything else tracks A. Where an override would be invalid (a geography without a grid, a route not solved there) it is clamped back and noted next to the scenario name.

The table shows the full route group behind each scenario: every route at that scenario's geography, year, grid, siting and sensitivity, cheapest first. The route A actually plots is highlighted and chipped A; if B sits in the same group its route is chipped B in place, otherwise B's group is appended below with a tinted background. Routes. H2-DRI-EAF, MOE and EW are zero-fossil; NG-H2-DRI-EAF is transitional (the optimiser blends H2 and gas DRI — the H2-share column shows how much iron came from hydrogen, and near ~0 it is flagged as economically the fossil route); NG-DRI-EAF is the natural-gas benchmark. LCOE is the electricity-system cost — renewables + battery + grid + transmission, storage included — per MWh generated; LCOH is €/MWh LHV of hydrogen (electrolyser + buffer + its electricity valued at LCOE). Values are near-optimal within input uncertainty — read rankings and ranges, not the last digit.
